# DCM ENUGU — USER MANUAL & SYSTEM GUIDE

Welcome to the official User Guide for **DCM Enugu (Directorate of Contact and Mobilization — Mobilizing for Good Governance)**.

---

## 1. Overview and Core Purpose

DCM Enugu serves as the central relationship-management, stakeholder-engagement, grassroots outreach, coordination, and mobilization structure across Enugu State.

### Core Distinction:
* **Contact:** Identifies key stakeholders (traditional/religious leaders, professional bodies, youth and women groups) and maintains long-term constructive relationships across wards and local government areas.
* **Mobilization:** Coordinates organized, lawful civic activities, meetings, conventions, and rallies while eliminating duplicate effort across administrative levels.

---

## 2. Public Application Features

1. **Homepage (`/`):** Custom hero section, mission, responsibilities, structure summary, and registration CTA.
2. **About Us (`/about`):** Detailed organizational identity, purpose, contact vs. mobilization breakdown, and structure hierarchy.
3. **Our Structure (`/structure`):** Interactive roster of State Executive, Zonal, and Local Government coordinators. Clicking any profile card opens a modal with full contact details and high-resolution face image expansion.
4. **INEC Officer Registration (`/register-inec-officer`):** Public portal allowing candidates to submit NIN, address, qualifications (OND, HND, Degree), position applied (SPO, PO, APO), and bank account details.

---

## 3. Central Settings Control Center (`/portal/admin/settings`)

Administrators have access to a central control center to manage:
* **Maintenance Mode:** Switch maintenance mode ON/OFF with confirmation. Select between two visitor experiences:
  * **Maintenance Page:** Polished centered card with logo, title, and maintenance message.
  * **Dark Blue Screen:** Minimal deep navy screen.
* **Hero Customization:** Update hero headlines, sub-taglines, CTA button labels, CTA target link, and background banner image.
* **Logo & Branding:** Customize brand name, sub-title, and custom logo icon.
* **Header & Footer:** Customize top header banner notice, physical secretariat address, phone number, email, and copyright line.
* **Theme Control:** Toggle between Light and Dark Mode.

---

## 4. Notifications & Broadcasts

* **Top Header Bell Icon:** Displays unread announcement counts and opens a drawer listing directorate broadcasts with "Mark all read" capabilities.
* **In-App Toast Alerts:** High-contrast toasts provide feedback for form submissions, settings updates, and administrative actions.
